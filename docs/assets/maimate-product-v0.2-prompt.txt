Use case: ui-mockup.
Create ONE high-resolution landscape product design presentation, approximately 3840x2160. It shows THREE readable flat mobile app screens side by side, separated by generous neutral gutters, no phone hardware, no rounded phone frames, no fake status bars, no browser chrome. Each screen has a tall mobile aspect ratio, all content fits without cropping. Three screens represent different flows in the same proposed McDonald's China app redesign called 麦Mate / MyMate. This is a concept with demo food and no live prices.
Overall art direction: playful but highly usable everyday food ordering. Treat this as professional native app UI, NOT a marketing landing page or a cinematic poster. Warm lightly tinted cream background, rich brown typography, restrained golden yellow accents, mostly flat functional open layouts. App top bars, modest headlines, useful touch controls, tab navigation. Gold accent area under 3% of overall interface. No giant headings, no white on yellow, no gradients, no purple/blue, no neon, no glows, no card-in-card, no equal three-feature-card grid, no fake testimonials or metrics, no emojis. Consistent monoline icons. Bricolage Grotesque-like Latin logo typography paired with clean Chinese sans-serif.
At top of entire composition place compact heading: 麦Mate，My mate。
Small subtitle: 产品架构概念 · 界面与餐品均为示意
Do not invent any other data. All prices must be — or 待核价. No countdowns, no coupon discount amounts.
TREASURY VISUAL LANGUAGE: original thin gold arch outlines resembling a sequence of open pantry gateways, some single and some double arches with a quiet subtle McDonald's golden arches connection. Warm matte, NOT elaborate palace sculpture. Different familiar food silhouettes visible through different small archways. The treasure metaphor conveys accessible variety and ownership. One small arch motif is carried across icons, saved meals and the selected result. Food rendering can be appetizing photorealistic but clearly illustrative.
